City of Bones

City of Bones is the first book in The Mortal Instruments series. It follows Clary Fray, a seemingly ordinary teenager who discovers she is part of a hidden world of Shadowhunters—warriors who hunt demons. After her mother disappears, Clary is thrust into a dangerous and magical underworld full of vampires, werewolves, and secrets about her own past.

Mocking Jay

"Mockingjay" is the third and final book in Suzanne Collins' The Hunger Games trilogy. It follows Katniss Everdeen as she becomes the symbol of resistance against the oppressive Capitol. After surviving the deadly Hunger Games, Katniss is thrust into the chaos of war as she must navigate her role as a leader in the rebellion. As the battle for freedom intensifies, Katniss struggles with love, loyalty, and her own identity, all while trying to survive in a world on the brink of destruction.

Lightning Thief

The Lightning Thief follows Percy Jackson, a 12-year-old boy who discovers he is the son of Poseidon, the Greek god of the sea. When Zeus’s lightning bolt is stolen, Percy is accused of the crime and embarks on a dangerous quest to find the thief and prevent a war among the gods. Along with his friends Annabeth Chase and Grover the satyr, Percy must face mythical creatures and gods, unravel family secrets, and discover his true destiny as a hero in this action-packed adventure.

A curse for True Love

A Curse for True Love is a darkly enchanting tale where love is both the prize and the punishment. With her memories lost and fate hanging by a thread, Evangeline must unravel deadly secrets and forbidden magic—before true love becomes her ultimate curse.

Catching Fire

Katniss Everdeen becomes the face of rebellion after surviving the Hunger Games. In a shocking twist, she is forced back into the arena for the Quarter Quell, facing new threats and deepening political unrest.

Curse of Shadows and Thorns

A cursed prince, a stubborn heroine, and a kingdom at stake—this dark fantasy romance blends magic, secrets, and the slow burn of forbidden love.

The Sunlit Man

A fugitive with mysterious powers races across a vibrant yet deadly world, unraveling ancient technology and the truth behind a crumbling society.

The Sea of Monsters

Percy Jackson sails into the Sea of Monsters to rescue a friend and retrieve the Golden Fleece, encountering mythical beasts and dark prophecy.

The Ballad of Never After

Evangeline Fox searches for her happily ever after while navigating dangerous bargains and ancient curses in a world where true love may be the most dangerous thing of all.

The Sun and the Star

Nico di Angelo and Will Solace venture into Tartarus to save a lost friend, testing their courage and love in the face of the darkest threats.

The Familiar

A young woman discovers her link to a magical creature and an ancient war, uncovering the mystery of her identity and the powers she must learn to control.

The Hunger Games

In a dystopian future, Katniss Everdeen volunteers for a brutal televised fight to the death, sparking a movement that could topple the regime.

The King of Battle and Blood

Forced to marry a powerful vampire king, a fierce warrior princess must navigate love, war, and a dangerous court where betrayal lurks in every shadow.

The Midnight Library

Between life and death lies a library of infinite possibilities. Nora Seed explores alternate versions of her life to find the one worth living.

The Sun and the Star

Nico and Will confront ancient fears and new responsibilities as they journey through the depths of the Underworld in search of redemption and healing.

The Titan's Curse

When Annabeth is captured, Percy and his friends must rescue her and the goddess Artemis while facing a rising threat that could destroy Olympus.

Yumi and the Nightmare Painter

Two artists from opposing worlds must share bodies and dreams to stop an ancient evil, discovering the power of creation and trust along the way.

The Atlas Six

Six gifted magicians are recruited by a secret society guarding ancient knowledge—but only five will survive the initiation. Betrayal is inevitable.
