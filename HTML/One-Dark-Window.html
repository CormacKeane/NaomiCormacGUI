Interview with the Vampire
Anne Rice

Interview with the Vampire is a gothic masterpiece that explores the dark and seductive world of vampires. ... Told through the eyes of Louis, a centuries-old vampire, the story delves into themes of immortality, love, and loss. As Louis recounts his transformation into a vampire by the enigmatic Lestat, readers are drawn into a haunting tale of betrayal, companionship, and the eternal struggle between humanity and monstrosity. Anne Rice's vivid prose and rich storytelling make this a timeless classic of the horror genre.
