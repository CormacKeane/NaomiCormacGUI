Bound by Fire and Scales
Julie Hardy

Bound by Fire and Scales is an epic fantasy tale of courage, destiny, and forbidden love. ... In a world where dragons and humans coexist in fragile harmony, a young warrior discovers a hidden power within herself that could change the fate of her people. As she embarks on a perilous journey to uncover the truth about her lineage, she must navigate treacherous alliances, ancient prophecies, and a love that defies all odds. With breathtaking world-building, intense battles, and a story that weaves together magic and emotion, this book is a spellbinding adventure that will leave readers captivated.

450 pages, Hardcover
