City of bones #1
Cassandra Clare


City of Bones by Cassandra Clare introduces readers to Clary Fray, an ordinary teenager who is suddenly thrust into a hidden world of demons, Shadowhunters, and dark secrets. When her mother is kidnapped, Clary discovers her true heritage and uncovers a dangerous world lurking just beneath the surface of New York City. With the help of a group of skilled warriors called Shadowhunters, Clary must navigate this magical realm and face unimaginable threats to save her loved ones.... But as she delves deeper into the mystery, she discovers that not everything — and everyone — is what it seems. Packed with action, intrigue, and romance, City of Bones is the thrilling first installment in The Mortal Instruments series, where destiny is written in the blood and nothing is ever as simple as it seems.

485 pages, Hardcover
First published March 27, 2007
