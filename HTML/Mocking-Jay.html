the hunger games

Mocking Jay #3
Suzanne Collins


War has stripped the world of color, and Katniss Everdeen is no longer a girl on fire—she’s a shadow burning from the inside out. Thrust into the heart of a rebellion she never asked for, she becomes a living weapon forged by trauma, tragedy, and televised lies. The Capitol crumbles, but so does everything human in her. Beneath the banners of freedom, puppeteers pull strings with bloodied hands. Allies become strangers. Truth is a minefield. Love is collateral. ...And hope? Hope is a currency spent far too easily in the rubble of what used to be home. This is not a victory march. It’s the slow disintegration of a soul made into a symbol. Mockingjay is the story of survival, yes—but it’s also the quiet, aching question that follows: what survives you?

390 pages, Hardcover
First published August 24, 2010
